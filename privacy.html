Your privacy, our care.

We keep things simple. Here’s exactly what we collect, why, and how you can control it — no legalese, just straight answers.

Last updated: March 2025

Yonder Garden Works (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates this journal for gardeners who want to grow with intention. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We’ve written it in plain language because we believe privacy should be clear.

1. What we collect

We only collect information you voluntarily give us. That includes:

  • Contact form data: your name, email address, and the message you send through our contact page.
  • Newsletter signup: your email address when you subscribe to “Field Notes.”
  • Usage data (anonymised): we use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which articles are helpful. We don’t track individual readers, and we never sell or share this data.

2. How we use your information

Your information helps us:

  • Reply to your questions, pitches, or corrections.
  • Send you our monthly-ish newsletter — but only if you ask us to.
  • Improve our content by understanding which topics readers find most useful (using aggregated, non-identifiable data).

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information with anyone. Ever.

The short version

We don’t sell your data. We don’t share it with advertisers. We only use it to talk with you and improve the journal.

3. Cookies & similar technologies

We use a minimal set of cookies to remember your preferences and to measure aggregate site traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time — the site will still work fine, though a few conveniences may be lost. For full details, see our Cookies Policy.

4. Third-party services

We keep third-party integrations to a minimum. Currently, we use:

  • Email delivery service — to send our newsletter. Only your email address is shared, and only for the purpose of delivery.
  • Privacy-focused analytics — to understand aggregate readership. No personal data is stored.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook pixels, or any ad-tracking scripts.

5. Data retention

Contact form submissions are kept for up to 24 months so we can reference past conversations. Newsletter subscriber data is kept until you unsubscribe. If you’d like us to delete your information sooner, just ask — we’ll do it within 7 days.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (e.g., unsubscribe from the newsletter).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@yondergardenworks.com. We’ll respond within 7 days.

7. Children’s privacy

Our content is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe we have inadvertently done so, please contact us and we’ll delete it promptly.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we’ll post a notice on our homepage and update the “last updated” date at the top of this page. We’ll never make retroactive changes that reduce your rights without your consent.
