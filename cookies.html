Cookies, clearly explained.

We use a tiny handful of cookies — only what’s needed to make the site work and to understand which articles help you most. No tracking, no ads, no surprises.

Last updated: March 2025

This Cookies Policy explains how Yonder Garden Works (“we”, “us”, “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website. We’ve kept it simple — because cookies shouldn’t require a law degree to understand.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your actions and preferences (like language or font size) over a period of time, so you don’t have to keep re-entering them whenever you come back.

They’re not programs, they can’t carry viruses, and they don’t give us access to your device.

2. How we use cookies

We use a minimal set of cookies — we like to think of them as the helpful garden markers of the web. They fall into two categories:

  • Essential cookies: necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic features like page navigation and access to secure areas. The website cannot function properly without these.
  • Analytics cookies: we use a privacy‑focused, aggregate analytics tool that sets a single cookie to help us understand which pages are most visited. We never track individual users, and the data is anonymised.

The short version

We don’t use advertising cookies, we don’t sell your data, and we don’t track you across other websites. Our analytics are aggregate‑only and privacy‑first.

3. The cookies we set

Here’s the full list — short and sweet:

Cookie name Type Purpose Duration
yonder_session Essential Maintains your session while you browse the site. It helps the site remember your preferences and keeps your visit secure. Session (expires when you close your browser)
yonder_prefs Essential Remembers lightweight preferences like your cookie consent choice and whether you’ve dismissed a notice. 1 year
yonder_analytics Analytics Anonymised, aggregate data about page visits and navigation. We use this only to understand which topics resonate. No personal identifiers are stored. 30 days

We do not use any third‑party advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, or cross‑site tracking technologies.

4. Third‑party cookies

We keep third‑party services to an absolute minimum. Currently, our newsletter delivery service may set a cookie when you interact with the subscription form — but that cookie is only used to prevent duplicate submissions and ensure the form works properly. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

If we ever add a third‑party service that sets cookies, we’ll update this page and, where required, ask for your consent.

5. Your choices

You have full control over cookies. Here’s how you can manage them:

  • Browser settings: most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Check your browser’s help section for instructions. Blocking all cookies may affect the functionality of this and other websites.
  • Opt‑out of analytics: because our analytics are anonymised and aggregate‑only, there’s no personal profile to opt out of. However, you can still block the yonder_analytics cookie via your browser if you prefer.
  • Do Not Track: we respect the Do Not Track (DNT) browser setting. When DNT is enabled, we automatically disable our analytics cookie.

6. Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes to how we use cookies, we’ll update this page and revise the “last updated” date at the top. We’ll also post a notice on our homepage so you’re always in the loop.

7. Contact

Questions about cookies or this policy? We’re happy to help.
