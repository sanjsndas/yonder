Terms of Privacy

Clear, plain-language terms for using Yonder Garden Works. We keep it simple: no ads, no data selling, and no nonsense.

Last updated: April 2025 Version 1.2 Applies to all visitors

By using Yonder Garden Works, you agree to the terms below. We've written them to be readable — not to hide behind legalese. If you have questions, reach out.

1. Who we are

Yonder Garden Works ("we", "us", "our") is an independent, reader-supported journal for gardeners. Our website is yondergardenworks.com (the "Site"). We are not a corporation; we're a small editorial team publishing field-tested knowledge.

These Terms of Privacy cover your use of the Site, our newsletter, and any content we publish. They do not cover third-party sites we may link to — please check their terms separately.

2. Your privacy, plainly

We collect as little as possible. When you visit the Site, our server may log standard technical information (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and basic analytics. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. Ever.

What we collect:

  • Email address — only if you subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Usage data — anonymous, aggregated page views to help us improve.
  • Comments or messages — if you contact us directly.

What we never do: sell your data, run targeted ads, or share your email with third parties.

For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

3. Newsletter & communications

If you subscribe to our field notes, we'll send you occasional emails with new guides, seasonal reminders, and editorial updates. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email.

We use a third-party email service to manage our list. That provider stores your email address on our behalf and is bound by strict data protection agreements. We never give them permission to contact you independently.

4. Cookies & tracking

We use a minimal set of cookies to remember your preferences and to understand which articles are most useful. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or invasive analytics.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Our Cookies Policy explains exactly what we use.

5. Editorial independence

Every guide, article, and field note on Yonder Garden Works is written by our team or invited contributors who actually garden. We do not accept sponsored content, paid product placements, or affiliate links that compromise our recommendations.

If we mention a tool, seed variety, or technique, it's because we've tested it or believe it's genuinely useful — not because someone paid us. Our only loyalty is to our readers and the soil.
